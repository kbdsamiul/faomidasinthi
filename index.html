01 About

Studying how crops cope with salt, drought and metal stress.

Faomida Sinthi is an agronomy researcher with an MSc from Sher-e-Bangla Agricultural University, Dhaka. Her work looks at how rice and Brassica campestris respond to salt, drought and metal stress, and how biostimulants and beneficial microbes can improve tolerance.

She has co-authored five journal articles and eight book chapters, and has presented her research at international conferences in India and Nepal. She also attended an internationally funded training school in Nepal with early career researchers from several countries.

She is looking for a PhD position where she can collaborate with international scientists.

02 Research interests

What I work on

01

Salt stress tolerance in rice

MSc thesis on nanophosphate fertilizer and the phosphate solubilizing bacterium Acinetobacter calcoaceticus.

02

Antioxidant defense and glyoxalase systems

Enzyme assays, lipid peroxidation, osmolyte accumulation and ion homeostasis under stress.

03

Seaweed extracts and biostimulants

Ascophyllum nodosum extract, alginic acid and selenium as protectants in rice and Brassica campestris.

04

Plant growth-promoting bacteria

Culture, screening, identification and preservation of beneficial microbes.

05

Metal and metalloid toxicity

Plant responses to cadmium and arsenic, alone and combined with salinity.

06

Climate data analysis

Bias correction, downscaling, ETCCDI indices and trend analysis.
